Z-Anatomy muscle and skeletal models
====================================

These adapted 3D muscle and skeletal meshes are derived from Z-Anatomy
(https://www.z-anatomy.com/), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Z-Anatomy includes
material derived from BodyParts3D, copyright The Database Center for Life
Science (DBCLS), also licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

The models were exported with the AJFUTURES/anatomy-engine pipeline,
decimated, Draco-compressed, and adapted to CorosLink's strength view. The
muscular_lite.glb and skeletal_lite.glb derivatives are distributed under the
same CC BY-SA 4.0 license:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
