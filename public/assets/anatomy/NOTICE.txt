Z-Anatomy muscle model
======================

This adapted 3D muscle mesh is derived from Z-Anatomy
(https://www.z-anatomy.com/), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Z-Anatomy includes
material derived from BodyParts3D, copyright The Database Center for Life
Science (DBCLS), also licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

The model was exported with the AJFUTURES/anatomy-engine pipeline, decimated,
Draco-compressed, grouped by structure, and adapted to CorosLink's strength
muscle taxonomy. This muscular_lite.glb derivative is distributed under the
same CC BY-SA 4.0 license:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
