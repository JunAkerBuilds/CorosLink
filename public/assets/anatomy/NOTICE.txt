Z-Anatomy anatomy models
========================

These adapted 3D muscle, connective-tissue, and skeletal meshes are exported
directly from Z-Anatomy
(https://www.z-anatomy.com/), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Z-Anatomy includes
material derived from BodyParts3D, copyright The Database Center for Life
Science (DBCLS), also licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

The models were exported from the upstream Blender atlas at revision
b16b4c2eb7de824722f5eef9d5f56c5569a3d640, then decimated, Draco-compressed,
and adapted to CorosLink's strength view. The muscular_lite.glb export retains
Z-Anatomy's original linea alba mesh. Both GLB derivatives are distributed
under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
