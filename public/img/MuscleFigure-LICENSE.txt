MuscleFigure.usdz
=================

C-LINK's muscle-map figure is adapted from:

  "Z-Anatomy - The libre 3D atlas of anatomy - CC-BY-SA 4.0"
  https://www.z-anatomy.com
  https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy

  "BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science - CC-BY-SA 2.1 Japan"
  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html

Changes: a selection of Z-Anatomy's muscles and bones only (no organs, nerves or the
NC-licensed reference models), joined into muscle groups, decimated for iPhone, re-oriented
and scaled. Built by scripts/muscle-figure/build_figure.py.
