## Z-Anatomy muscle model

The 3D muscle mesh used by the experimental Strength v2 view is derived from
[Z-Anatomy](https://www.z-anatomy.com/), which is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Z-Anatomy includes material derived from BodyParts3D, copyright The Database
Center for Life Science (DBCLS), also licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

CorosLink uses the export pipeline and structure metadata from
[AJFUTURES/anatomy-engine](https://github.com/AJFUTURES/anatomy-engine). The
source FBX was Draco-compressed, decimated, grouped by structure, recolored at
runtime, and adapted to CorosLink's strength muscle taxonomy. The resulting
`muscular_lite.glb` derivative is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
