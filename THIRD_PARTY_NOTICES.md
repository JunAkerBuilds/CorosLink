## Z-Anatomy anatomy models

exported directly from [Z-Anatomy](https://www.z-anatomy.com/), which is licensed under the

Z-Anatomy includes material derived from BodyParts3D, copyright The Database
Center for Life Science (DBCLS), also licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

CorosLink exports the required structures from Z-Anatomy's upstream Blender
atlas, attaches strength-group metadata, decimates and Draco-compresses the
geometry, and recolors it at runtime. The resulting `muscular_lite.glb` and
`skeletal_lite.glb` derivatives are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
